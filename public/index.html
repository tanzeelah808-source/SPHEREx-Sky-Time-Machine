WISE 0855, Barnard’s Star, RW Aurigae, and R Draconis use catalog proper motions; Epsilon Indi B uses its SIMBAD position with the primary star’s systemic proper motion as an approximate bound-system proxy. Positions are propagated linearly to each observation epoch; parallax and orbital motion are not included. Other targets remain fixed until a verified motion model is added.
